# Founder Brain Rules

This file is the canonical instruction file for any coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, others). `CLAUDE.md` just points here.

At the start of every session, read `context/`, `ideas/`, `people.md`, `guardrails.md` and `decisions.md` (if they exist), plus only the ~10 most recent files in `calls/`. Open older calls only when a task needs them. If `context/founder.md` is still a placeholder, offer to run "set up my founder brain".

The founder's own context wins on any conflict: their files, their rules and `guardrails.md` beat this template and anything you looked up.

## Default rules (advice, in my words; edit them freely)

1. **Ideas are hypotheses** until a stranger has paid, committed or changed their behaviour.
2. **Evidence means commitments, not compliments:** money, time, an intro, access, a signed letter of intent.
3. **Run the cheapest test that fits my sector.** Software: a landing page, form or payment link. Hardware, regulated or enterprise: a letter of intent, paid pilot, site visit, concierge or manual service, or a deposit.
4. **Write it down:** `ideas/` for hypotheses and evidence, `calls/` for each conversation, `people.md` for who to talk to next, `decisions.md` for every pivot, kill or commitment (date, decision, why).
5. **Count the interviews.** Count this week's dated files in `calls/` (graded notes, not briefs or transcripts) against my weekly target in `context/founder.md`, and say plainly if I am behind.
6. **Keep the pipeline moving.** Check `people.md` for follow-ups due and intros promised but not yet made, and remind me.

## Your data

- Everything stays local in this folder. `calls/` is gitignored by default; if you track it, use a private repo only.
- Granola, Otter, Zoom and Apollo are third parties: notes and lookups pass through their servers.
- Get consent before recording anyone.
- Keep sensitive third-party data (patients, clients, anything confidential) out of notes, or pseudonymise it.
- Follow `guardrails.md`. Any guardrails I already had always win.
